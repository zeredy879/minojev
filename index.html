What minojev does

Typed primitives

Choice, boolean, and score questions return distributions with stable semantics and validation limits.

Dynamic candidates

The same head handles 2 to 255 options supplied at request time.

Zero decoding

Decisions come from one forward pass; every record reports zero generated tokens.

Parallel judgment

Independent questions over shared state are batched without cross-question attention.

Shared-state reuse

One KV-cache prefill per distinct state, then branches for every question and candidate.

Offline reproduction

A built-in tiny backbone and byte tokenizer train from scratch on a laptop CPU with exact teacher targets.

Quickstart

minojev synth --out data/train.jsonl --count 512 --seed 17 --split train
minojev train --train data/train.jsonl --dev data/dev.jsonl \
  --output-dir runs/synth --steps 800 --device cpu
minojev score --checkpoint runs/synth/checkpoint \
  --input examples/decisions.jsonl --output results.jsonl --mode reuse
